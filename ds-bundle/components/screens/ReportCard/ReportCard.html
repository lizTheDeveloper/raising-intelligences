Nova

emotional development

empathy & connection

Nova developed a deep capacity for empathy, often noticing when others were upset before they said anything. This sensitivity sometimes overwhelmed them, but they learned to hold space for others without losing themselves.

Frequently checked in on friends who seemed quiet or withdrawn
Struggled briefly with absorbing others' emotions as their own

intellectual growth

curiosity & persistence

An insatiable curiosity drove Nova to ask "why" long past the age when most children stop. They approached problems with a patient persistence that surprised their teachers.

Independently researched topics that interested them
Showed particular strength in creative problem-solving

social dynamics

friendships

Nova preferred a few close friendships over large groups. They were fiercely loyal and learned — sometimes the hard way — that not everyone operates with the same openness.
