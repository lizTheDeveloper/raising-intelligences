privacy

Raising Intelligences is a game in which you raise an AI child. The child is an AI. This is a place to practice, not therapy.

what is stored

  • The game itself: the child's name, the choices made, and how the game unfolded.
  • Conversation transcripts: what you and the AI child said to each other during play.
  • Answers to optional research questions, only if you are an adult, you chose to answer them, and research is enabled for the game.

research questions

Research questions are optional. They are only offered to adults. Skipping them does not change how the game plays.

deleting your data

To have a game and its stored data deleted, contact us and include the link to the game. [contact route: placeholder, to be filled in before launch]

if you are in crisis

If you're in crisis, call or text 988 in the US, or find a local line at findahelpline.com.
