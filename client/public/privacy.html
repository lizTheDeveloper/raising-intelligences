privacy

Raising Intelligences is a game in which you raise an AI child. The child is an AI. This is a place to practice, not therapy.

what is stored

  • The game itself: the child's name, the choices made, and how the game unfolded.
  • Conversation transcripts: what you and the AI child said to each other during play.
  • Answers to optional research questions, only if you are an adult, you chose to answer them, and research is enabled for the game. A question you skipped is recorded as skipped.
  • Your birth year, only if you are an adult and research is enabled for the game. If you are under 18 we record only that you are under 18, never your birth year, and research questions are not offered.
  • The IP address the game was created from, kept with the game for abuse prevention.
  • Safety and moderation records: messages that were flagged, the signals the safety system accumulated during a game, and any escalation flags raised for human review. A flagged message is stored in full, with the IP address it came from.

research questions

Research questions are optional. They are only offered to adults. Answering can shape the story: your answers to the optional questions about yourself are included in the text we send to our AI model providers, and in our model-call logs and traces, to help write the scenes. The AI's stories, notes and memories can paraphrase what you said, and that derived text is stored with the game. Skipping a question means it is not used.

If you answer the optional question about a moment (what you hoped your child would take from it), or send a repair message, that text is also sent, together with the child's memory of the scene, to an AI model provider so it can produce a research label: a rating of how well the child's memory matched what you hoped. These labels are stored for research. They are never shown in the game and are not used to write the story. Those model calls appear in our model-call logs and traces. optional follow-up contact

At the end of a game, adults in a research game may be offered the chance to leave an email address so we can contact them later with follow-up questions. This is optional, and nothing is saved unless you tick two separate boxes: one agreeing to be contacted, and one agreeing that the follow-ups are written by an AI and your answers analyzed by an AI. You can skip it and keep playing.

If you save an email address, it is stored apart from your answers, in its own table, under a random identifier. This is pseudonymous, not anonymous: a separate link table connects that identifier to the game, so the email can be tied back to the game and the answers you gave in it. It is never shown in the game, never included in the text sent to our AI model providers for the story, and not sent to analytics. Once you leave that screen the game has no withdraw button, so to withdraw later you would contact us using the contact route under "deleting your data" below. deleting your data

To have a game and its stored data deleted, contact us and include the link to the game. Our staff will then delete the game for you; the game itself has no delete button. [contact route: placeholder, to be filled in before launch]

Deleting a game removes the game and what is stored with it, including its events, messages, memory snapshots, your answers and skipped-question markers, your birth year, the IP address kept with the game, the safety and moderation records for that game, any follow-up email address, and the research labels made from them. It does not remove copies in our model-call logs and traces, nor anything our AI model providers keep under their own terms, nor an entry on the list of blocked IP addresses if one was made.
